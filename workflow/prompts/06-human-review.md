Conduct a human review before collecting data or testing hypotheses:

- Check that every theory has coherent scoped laws, traceable evidence, and an
  explicit account of conflicting and unaccounted evidence.
- Check that each prediction follows from its cited law, has observable signals,
  and can be contradicted under its stated strong-test requirement.
- Check every falsification test: its null and alternative hypotheses, required
  data, relevance, implementability, and missing information.
- Pay special attention to tests marked `partial` or `blocked` and to their
  `missing_information`.
- Keep a workflow marked `ready` separate from empirical validation.

If data have already been collected, record a separate validation result as
`support`, `contradict`, or `no_evidence`. Do not infer p-values, e-values, or
theory probabilities from this preflight review.
